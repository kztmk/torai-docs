# About the Subscription

Torai is a subscription service so that we can continue providing it reliably.

Membership fees may increase as hardware and maintenance costs rise. In principle, however, we aim to let existing members renew at the rate that applied when they joined.

## Subscription prices

### Credit card

| Term    | Price |
| ------- | ----- |
| Monthly | 8 USD |

:::info
Since we use Stripe, we never receive your card information. You can also easily cancel your subscription through Stripe.
:::

## How to subscribe

Open [Torai](https://torai.try-try.com/auth/signin).

![Torai home page](/img/english-manual/torai-subscription/subsc01.jpeg)

Sign in with your Google Account.

Torai runs within your Google account:

- Data is stored in your Google Sheets.
- Programs run through Apps Script linked to your Google Sheets.
- Media files are stored in your Google Drive.

Accept the terms.

![Accept the terms](/img/english-manual/torai-subscription/subsc02.jpeg)

After accepting, the Subscription page opens.

![Select a subscription](/img/english-manual/torai-subscription/subsc03.jpeg)

### Credit-card subscription

You can cancel at any time. There is no refund, but you can continue using Torai until the end of the current billing period. The next renewal will not be processed.

Click **Apply by credit card** to open the payment page.

![Select a subscription](/img/english-manual/torai-subscription/subsc03.jpeg)

Payments are processed through Stripe, so we never receive your credit-card details.

![Stripe checkout](/img/english-manual/torai-subscription/subsc04.jpeg)

After payment, the subscription completion page appears. Click the button to open your Stripe customer page, where you can also cancel.

![Subscription complete](/img/english-manual/torai-subscription/subsc05.jpeg)

## How to cancel

You can cancel Torai at any time and continue using it until the end of the paid term.

Unless you delete your account under **Profile > Account Information**, you can restart at any time.

### Credit card

Open **Profile > Subscription**, then click **Manage or review subscription**.

![Subscription information](/img/english-manual/torai-subscription/subsc06.jpeg)

Your personal Stripe page opens. Click **Cancel subscription**.

![Cancel subscription](/img/english-manual/torai-subscription/subsc-cancel01.jpeg)

Stripe asks for a reason. Click **Continue to cancellation**.

![Cancellation reason](/img/english-manual/torai-subscription/subsc-cancel02.jpeg)

Click **Cancel subscription**.

![Confirm cancellation](/img/english-manual/torai-subscription/subsc-cancel03.jpeg)

Confirm that the plan is cancelled. You will not be charged at the next renewal.

![Cancellation complete](/img/english-manual/torai-subscription/subsc-cancel04.jpeg)
![Cancellation complete](/img/english-manual/torai-subscription/subsc-cancel05.jpeg)
