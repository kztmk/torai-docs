# Prepare a Google Account

Torai uses Google Sheets, Google Apps Script, and Google Drive to manage post data, images, videos, and data collected by X Marketing.

![How Torai connects to Google services](/img/english-manual/torai-auto-posting-flow-en.png)

## What you need

- A Google Account
- Permission to create Google Sheets
- Permission to deploy Apps Script
- Enough Google Drive storage for images and videos

## Important notes

Google Sheets stores scheduled posts and results as well as responders, the latest metrics for each post, daily snapshots, and X Marketing run history. Apps Script runs automated publishing and the daily X Marketing collection job on Google's servers.

If you want to use a separate Google Account for posting, keep a record of which account is associated with each set of X API keys, Google Sheet, and Torai configuration.

:::caution
Responder names, reaction details, CRM notes, post text, and post metrics are also stored in your Google Sheet. Review its sharing settings before granting access to anyone else.
:::
